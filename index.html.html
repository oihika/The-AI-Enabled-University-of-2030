Human–AI Collaboration & Ethical Governance

The proposed ecosystem follows six principles.

01 — Augment Human Capability

Use AI to improve human performance wherever possible.

02 — Retain Accountability

Identifiable human responsibility remains for consequential decisions.

03 — Risk-Based Automation

The higher the potential harm, the stronger the human oversight.

04 — Transparency

Users should understand when and how AI affects them.

05 — Evolve Human Work

Redesign roles when tasks are automated rather than simply removing people.

06 — Reskilling by Design

AI deployment should include workforce-transition planning.
